## Mini-Project: Bella Beauty Salon Assistant

Bella is a mock beauty salon AI that helps customers check existing appointments and answer general questions. It uses an AppointmentLookup tool to search sample client appointments and a local model to respond to other questions. In testing, Bella gave a helpful answer when asked about shipping and correctly explained that shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. However, the chatbot also accepted incorrect information from a customer who claimed the store now closed at 11 PM, even though its original instructions said 7 PM. Appointment lookups and requests to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments stay in the code because they need reliable information and may require a human team member. This helps prevent the chatbot from making up appointment details or taking actions it cannot actually perform.
